Our Story

Built on one simple idea: pets deserve calm, unhurried care

Willow Creek opened its doors in 2014 with a single exam room and a big idea — that veterinary visits didn't have to be stressful for pets or their people. Over a decade later, we've grown into a full-service hospital, but that founding idea hasn't changed. Every appointment still starts with patience, and every treatment plan is still built around what's right for your family, not a clock.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before your visit.

Please bring any prior medical records, a list of current medications, and your pet on a secure leash or in a carrier. If it's a first visit for a puppy or kitten, a fresh stool sample is helpful for parasite screening.
Fasting is only required for procedures involving sedation or anesthesia, such as surgery or certain dental cleanings. For routine exams and vaccinations, your pet can eat normally beforehand.
Yes — we're partnered with major providers including Trupanion and Healthy Paws, and we're happy to submit paperwork on your behalf. We also accept CareCredit and offer in-house wellness plans.
Difficulty breathing, uncontrolled bleeding, suspected poisoning, seizures, inability to stand, or trauma from an accident all warrant an immediate call. When in doubt, call our 24/7 line and our team will guide you.
Plan for about 30–45 minutes for a new patient exam, which gives us time for a full history, physical exam, and an unhurried conversation about any concerns you have.
